Report an issue

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and build number, the ticker, and the steps that led to the issue. Describe what you expected and what happened. If you include a screenshot, remove private notes and other personal information first. Never send passwords, verification codes, or signing credentials.

Research is taking a while

The free research service may need about a minute to wake up. Keep the app open while it loads. If a request fails, use Retry after checking your connection. Previously saved research and notes remain available while the service is unavailable.

A company or figure is unavailable

This beta supports US-GAAP companies with 10-K filings and USD financial data. Some search results or figures may not be supported. Check reporting dates and source details. No demonstration data substitutes for missing research, and AI interpretation is currently disabled.

Saved reports and notes

Your library is stored on the device where you saved it. There is no account or cross-device sync. Original SEC links need an internet connection; retained excerpts can be read offline. Deleting a report deletes its notes. Avoid uninstalling the app as a troubleshooting step: clearing app storage may erase your library.

Privacy questions

Read our privacy policy or email the address above. We cannot recover a local library that we do not hold.

TickerBrief provides research for understanding, not personalized investment advice or trading instructions. The beta is distributed privately; this page does not provide a public tester invitation.
