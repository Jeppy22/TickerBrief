This policy covers the TickerBrief app, its research service, and these privacy and support pages.

Effective September 16, 2026

Research saved on your device

Watchlists, saved report versions, retained public-source excerpts, and personal notes are stored on your device. The app does not send this library to our backend or an AI provider. There is no app account or cloud synchronization.

You can remove watchlist entries, edit notes, and delete saved reports in the app. Deleting a report also removes its associated notes. Your library remains until you remove it or app storage is cleared; uninstalling may erase it. Device backups are controlled by your operating system and backup settings. We cannot restore a library we do not hold.

When you request live research

The app sends your company search text or requested ticker over HTTPS to our research service, hosted by Render. Render and the service receive network information needed to handle the request, such as your IP address and request headers. Do not put personal information into company searches.

The backend retrieves and caches public SEC filings and company data. Its application code does not create personal accounts, store a per-user search history, or accept uploads of your notes or saved library. Cached public reports are separate from the research saved on your device.

Hosting and operational logs

Our deployment configuration disables the application web server's routine access log. Operational, error, and infrastructure security logs can still exist. Disabling that access log does not mean Render receives no information or retains no logs.

Render's published dashboard log retention depends on the workspace plan. We have not verified a fixed deletion period for all provider-held infrastructure records, and do not promise that all hosting data is deleted after a particular number of days. See Render's privacy policy for its handling of personal information.

These public web pages

GitHub Pages hosts these pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including when you are not signed in. Its retention depends on the purpose and applicable obligations; we do not control or promise a fixed deletion time. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, external fonts, or embedded media. We do not set tracking cookies on these pages.

External sources

Opening a source link connects your browser to the SEC website. The SEC receives information about that visit under its own privacy and security policy. Reading a saved excerpt does not require opening its original link.

AI, advertising, and tracking

AI interpretation is disabled in this beta. Research requests are not sent to a model provider. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDKs, brokerage connections, or cross-app tracking. If AI processing is introduced later, this policy will be updated to explain that change before it is enabled.

TestFlight and support

When you test through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the app provider. This can include your name, email address, device details, and testing activity. Feedback you submit through TestFlight is also shared. We use this information to investigate problems and improve the beta; we do not share TestFlight data with third parties. Apple's TestFlight privacy information explains its collection and retention, including one year for beta feedback and potentially longer for crash and usage data while bugs are resolved.

If you email us, we receive your address, message, and any attachments through our email provider. We use support messages and beta feedback to answer you and resolve issues, retaining them as needed for those purposes and any applicable obligations. No fixed automatic deletion schedule has been established for our support correspondence. Avoid sending passwords, financial-account details, or private notes you do not want to share.

Questions, requests, and changes

Contact us to ask about this policy or request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold. We cannot directly delete data stored only on your device or control providers' own records. This page will show an updated effective date when the policy changes.
